Chapter One

A Letter to Subway

To the team at Subway — you run one of the world's best-known brands from two headquarters, Shelton and Miami, and you serve franchisees around the globe. That means a steady rhythm of early flights, visiting partners and events that have to run on time.

Our family has spent nearly two decades making sure travel days like those begin and end calmly. Our home base in North Haven is a short drive from your Shelton campus.

When your people ride with us, they are not simply being driven; they are carrying your name. So we arrive early, present immaculately, and answer the phone ourselves, day and night.

We would be honoured to earn a place as your ground transportation partner — beginning, if you will allow it, with a single trip.

Manmohan Komal

Chief Executive Officer · Absolute Transportation

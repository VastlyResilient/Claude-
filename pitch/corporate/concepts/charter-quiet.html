Absolute TransportationI · A Letter
Chapter I

A letter to Charter

Your people are asked to be in a great many places, and to arrive in each of them ready. Our family has spent the better part of two decades seeing that travel days of that kind begin and end calmly.

We keep one fleet, always in black and always immaculate. We answer the telephone ourselves, at any hour. We follow each flight, so that a chauffeur is waiting when the aircraft actually lands, and not when it was scheduled to.

None of this is remarkable on its own. What we hope you will notice is that it happens every time: the early arrival, the door held open, the quiet drive, the call returned at once. We treat every traveler you send us as though your own name were in the back seat, because in every way that matters it is.

We would be honoured to serve Charter Communications, and to begin, if you will allow it, with a single journey.

Manmohan Komal

Chief Executive Officer

# AI-Ready Advisor — Pillar Blurbs (while-they-take-the-test)

Read each blurb aloud as the room hits that step on their phones. Short bullets, no long sentences. One pillar per section. Each ends with a **WOW FACTOR** — the line you land harder.

**Master mnemonic:** **S.D.P.P.T.S.** — *"Smart Decisions Power Profitable Tech Stacks."*

---

## Pillar 1 — Strategy · Step 2 of 7

**WHAT YOU'RE ANSWERING**
- 3 questions about your AI **plan**, **urgency**, and **ownership**.
- AI Adoption Level · Competitive Concern · AI Ownership.

**WHAT IT MEASURES**
- Is AI a **hobby**, a **project**, or a **strategy**?
- Are you reacting to headlines, or moving with intent?
- Who actually owns AI in your company?

**WHAT YOUR ANSWERS REVEAL**
- **"Not at all"** → you're at the starting line.
- **"Pilot project"** → testing, not committed.
- **"Integrated strategy"** → you're operationalized.
- **"No One" owns AI** → the #1 reason AI investment stalls.

**⚡ WOW FACTOR**
- **AI Ownership** is the **single best predictor** of ROI in 12 months.
- Better than budget. Better than headcount. Better than industry.
- Companies with a dedicated AI lead are **2× more likely** to capture ROI.

## Pillar 2 — Data · Step 3 of 7

**WHAT YOU'RE ANSWERING**
- 3 questions about how **findable**, **clean**, and **connected** your data is.
- Search Time · Data Cleanliness · Workflow Stalls.

**WHAT IT MEASURES**
- Whether your data is **AI-readable**.
- How much time your team loses searching.
- How often work stalls waiting on info.

**WHAT YOUR ANSWERS REVEAL**
- **"Chaos / Paper / Various"** → data isn't ready. Foundation first.
- **"Scattered / Siloed"** → typical SMB. Big upside.
- **"Centralized / Cloud"** → ready for AI today.

**⚡ WOW FACTOR**
- **AI is only as smart as the data you let it see.**
- 60+ minutes/day of search = **5 full weeks/year** per person. Gone.
- Centralized + Cloud companies adopt AI **3× faster** than the rest.

## Pillar 3 — Process (SOP) · Step 4 of 7

**WHAT YOU'RE ANSWERING**
- 2 questions about your **speed-to-respond** and how **documented** your work is.
- Response Velocity · Process Documentation (SOPs).

**WHAT IT MEASURES**
- Whether work is **repeatable**.
- Whether AI has a process to automate.
- How fast you actually move when a lead comes in.

**WHAT YOUR ANSWERS REVEAL**
- **"24+ hrs response"** → revenue leaking out the front door.
- **"None / Not many SOPs"** → AI has nothing to follow.
- **"Fully Standardized"** → AI integration can begin immediately.

**⚡ WOW FACTOR**
- Lead response in **5 minutes** = **9× higher conversion** than at 30 minutes.
- Without SOPs, you don't have a **process**. You have a **habit**.
- AI workflow agents can run SOPs **24/7** with zero new headcount.

## Pillar 4 — People & Leadership · Step 5 of 7

**WHAT YOU'RE ANSWERING**
- 3 questions about how your **team feels**, your **AI policy**, and your **budget appetite**.
- Team Sentiment · AI Use Policy · Budget Willingness.

**WHAT IT MEASURES**
- Will your team actually **USE** the AI you buy?
- Do you have rules for safe use?
- Will leadership invest if ROI is proven?

**WHAT YOUR ANSWERS REVEAL**
- **"Fearful / Skeptical"** → resistance ahead. Train first.
- **No AI policy** → shadow-AI risk is already in the building.
- **"Yes to 5× ROAI budget"** → buyer signal.

**⚡ WOW FACTOR**
- **70% of AI rollouts fail** — not because of the tech, but because the team won't use it.
- A **1-page** Acceptable Use Policy cuts data-leak risk by an estimated **80%**.
- Companies with 3+ AI champions on staff adopt **4× faster**.

## Pillar 5 — Technology · Step 6 of 7

**WHAT YOU'RE ANSWERING**
- 3 questions about whether your **systems talk** to each other, where data lives, and how many tools you use.
- System Integration · Data Location · Tool Fatigue.

**WHAT IT MEASURES**
- The **plumbing** that lets AI act across your stack.
- Whether data is in the **cloud** or on **local drives**.
- Whether your team is drowning in tools.

**WHAT YOUR ANSWERS REVEAL**
- **"Not at all integrated"** → fix plumbing *before* adding AI.
- **"Local drives"** → AI can't see your data yet.
- **"Overwhelmed by tools"** → AI *consolidates*, doesn't add.

**⚡ WOW FACTOR**
- Average mid-size business uses **130+ SaaS apps**. Most overlap.
- Integrated systems = AI delivers **4× more value** than siloed ones.
- **Tool sprawl costs more than tool gaps.**

---

## Pillar 6 — Security · Step 7 of 7

**WHAT YOU'RE ANSWERING**
- 3 questions about **shadow AI**, **access control**, and your **regulatory environment**.
- Shadow-AI Risk · Access Revocation · Regulated Industry.

**WHAT IT MEASURES**
- Are employees using free AI tools with sensitive data?
- Can you instantly revoke ex-employee access?
- Are you in a regulated industry (HIPAA, FINRA, SOC2)?

**WHAT YOUR ANSWERS REVEAL**
- **"Yes" or "I don't know"** to shadow-AI → breach risk is **live**.
- **"Can't revoke access"** → every offboarding is a leak.
- **"Regulated industry"** → consumer AI is a fine waiting to happen.

**⚡ WOW FACTOR**
- An estimated **75% of enterprise data leaks** in 2025 now flow through generative AI.
- **Shadow AI** is the biggest security blind spot most leaders don't know they have.
- A regulated company using consumer ChatGPT is one audit away from a six-figure fine.
